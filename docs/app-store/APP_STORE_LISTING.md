## Description

Bring your wedding plans together with WeddingWin.

Explore wedding vendors and venues, create your wedding website, and keep conversations with wedding professionals in one place.

For couples
- Find vendors and venues for your wedding.
- Create and manage your wedding website.
- Keep private conversations with vendors together.
- Scan participating vendors' QR codes and track your QR Bingo progress.
- Choose whether to enter an available vendor draw with a simple Yes or No.

For wedding professionals
- Access your vendor dashboard and manage your profile.
- Reply to couples through private messages.
- Set up and manage your optional vendor draw.

QR Bingo and vendor draws are available at participating events when scanning is open. Scanning records your visit; draw entry is optional and subject to eligibility and the Official Rules. Event admission is separate.

A WeddingWin account and an internet connection are required for account features.
