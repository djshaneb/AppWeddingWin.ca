## Current production QR flow

Couples provide contact details and accept the current QR Bingo agreement before scanning. During organiser-authorised scanning, an enabled vendor may offer its named draw with a short recorded prize summary and value. Read more opens full recorded conditions and dates. Yes submits the named entry under the accepted terms; No keeps scan progress without entering. A scan never enters automatically. The separate screenshot fixture does not provide the production Yes path.
