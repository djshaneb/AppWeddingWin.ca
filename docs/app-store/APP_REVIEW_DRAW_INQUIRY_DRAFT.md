**Subject:** Clarify guideline 5.3.1 for independently operated vendor draws at an in-person wedding show

Hello App Review,

Please clarify guideline 5.3.1 for apps that provide draw tools to independent vendors at an in-person event. We are preparing WeddingWin Canada (Apple ID 6806603211, bundle ca.weddingwin.app) for a Canada-only release.

At an in-person wedding show, a vendor activates its optional draw and records its prize details. A signed-in couple scans that vendor's QR code, sees the named vendor and prize information, and chooses Yes or No to enter. The app records that choice and provides vendor entry, winner-selection, verification and explicit Send controls for result/contact instructions. The owner confirms that vendors independently run their draws; WeddingWin provides the software.

We understand that guideline 5.3.1 requires developer sponsorship. Existing rules use “limited platform sponsor” wording for WeddingWin, but that wording does not establish actual developer operation or oversight. We want the rules and app behavior to reflect the real arrangement accurately.

Please add guidance explaining whether 5.3.1 permits this independently operated, in-person workflow and what responsibility the app developer must have. If these entry and result tools require developer oversight rather than independent vendor operation, clarification of the required responsibilities would help us make the necessary business and app changes before submission.

Thank you.
