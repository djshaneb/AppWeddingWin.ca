Enter an Enabled Vendor Draw with Its QR Code

When the organizer has opened QR scanning, including early access, eligible couples can scan a participating vendor's QR code and choose whether to enter when that vendor's draw is on. This page explains the process; it cannot be used to submit a new entry.

How to enter

  1. Read and agree to the QR Bingo Terms and Draw Rules before scanning.
  2. Wait until the organizer opens QR scanning and the vendor turns its draw on.
  3. Sign in to your WeddingWin couple account and scan that vendor's QR code.
  4. When asked whether to enter that named vendor's draw, choose Yes to enter under your prior agreement or No to keep only the scan.

The QR entry is the digital replacement for a paper ballot. Scanning records QR Bingo progress, but does not enter a draw automatically. Choosing No leaves you out of that vendor's draw. Early-access scans do not claim a booth visit during the show. One valid entry is allowed per eligible couple per named vendor draw.

The September 11, 2026 entry timing policy update (2026-09-11-vendor-enabled-entry) allows this entry offer during organizer-authorized early scanning. The displayed entry closing time, scheduled winner-draw time, prize, and other conditions still apply.
