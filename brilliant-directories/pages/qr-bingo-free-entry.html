Vendor Draw Entry Is Available at the Wedding Show

Vendor draws are available only to eligible couples attending the wedding show in person. This address is kept so older links still lead somewhere useful, but it cannot be used to submit a new entry.

How to enter

  1. Visit a participating vendor's booth at the wedding show.
  2. Sign in to your WeddingWin couple account and scan that vendor's QR code.
  3. Review the vendor's draw and choose whether to enter.

The QR entry is the digital replacement for a paper ballot. Scanning records the booth visit and QR Bingo progress, but does not enter a draw automatically. After scanning, choose whether to enter that vendor's draw. One valid entry is allowed per eligible couple per named vendor draw.
