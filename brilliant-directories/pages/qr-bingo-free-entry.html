This is a draw entry only

This public method does not require a WeddingWin account, ticket, show attendance, booth visit, or QR scan. It submits one entry request for the specific named vendor draw only. It does not record a booth visit, add a QR scan, fill or complete a QR Bingo card, unlock Bingo progress, or provide any other scan-based credit or benefit.

Before submitting

Review the WeddingWin Vendor Prize-Draw Official Rules and the public disclosure for the specific participating vendor. That disclosure must show the named vendor, number of prizes, each prize and its restrictions, approximate retail value or maximum savings in Canadian dollars, eligible region, opening and closing time, scheduled draw time, one-entry limit, odds basis, and skill-testing-question requirement. A percentage-off prize must also identify the qualifying service or package, maximum savings cap, expiry, booking requirements, and material exclusions. If those details are unavailable or the draw has closed, the draw is not open and this form cannot create an eligible entry.

How the request is handled

Identify the exact vendor and wedding-show event, provide the requested contact details, and complete the age-of-majority, residency, exclusion, and current-rules confirmations. Wedding Win will validate that the named vendor draw is configured and open, reconcile this request with any matching QR-method entry, and enforce one valid entry per eligible couple per vendor draw regardless of method. If the request is accepted, Wedding Win will give the named vendor the entrant information and entry/consent evidence expressly authorized below for administration of this specific draw and that vendor's own wedding-related marketing. WeddingWin applies account and normalized-email duplicate controls, but those technical controls do not prove that two different identifiers belong to the same couple. Using another account or email, submitting twice, or using both methods is prohibited and does not make a valid extra chance; associated entries may be voided and require reselection.

Eligible entries from this form and the QR method use the same closing time, eligibility rules, random pool, and odds. A submission receipt confirms only that the request was received; eligibility remains subject to validation and the Official Rules. The original Form 354 submission time—not the later administrator processing time—determines whether the request met the closing deadline.

Wedding Win Inc. acts only as the technical platform and administrator for the named vendor's promotion; it is not the named vendor-promotion sponsor, operator, or prize provider and does not supply, guarantee, insure, or fulfil the vendor's prize. Wedding Win remains responsible for its own technology, security, privacy, representations, administrative conduct, and every non-waivable duty or liability. Apple Inc. is not a sponsor, operator, prize provider, or administrator and is not involved in the draw.

Privacy

By submitting an entry request, I agree that Wedding Win Inc. may share my name, email address, phone number if provided, wedding date if provided, and entry/consent evidence with the named vendor to administer this specific draw and for that vendor's own wedding-related offers and promotions. The vendor must identify itself, safeguard the information, honour applicable unsubscribe or consent-withdrawal requests, and comply with applicable privacy and commercial-message laws. A QR scan alone does not share contact information with a vendor. This acknowledgement applies only to the named vendor draw selected below.
