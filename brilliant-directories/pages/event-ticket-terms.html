Version: 2026-09-01-niagara-ticket-sharing

These Event Admission and Ticket Terms (the “Event Terms”) apply to tickets and attendance for a wedding show or other event organized by Wedding Win Inc. (“Wedding Win”). They apply whether a ticket is free, paid, VIP, complimentary, or issued through a promotion. The general WeddingWin Terms of Use and Privacy Policy also apply to WeddingWin accounts, the app, and website services.

1. Admission options and prices

General admission is free when obtained in advance while the free ticket allocation remains available. VIP options are paid. A visitor without an advance general-admission ticket must buy admission at the door. Capacity, door pricing, VIP benefits, taxes, fees, and availability may vary by event and will be disclosed in the applicable ticket offer or checkout before an order is completed.

A free or paid ticket permits admission only for the event, date, time, and admission level shown on that ticket, subject to venue capacity, safety requirements, these Event Terms, and applicable law. A ticket has no cash value and may not be resold for profit without Wedding Win’s written permission.

2. Vendor prize draws are separate from admission

Buying or obtaining a ticket, choosing VIP admission, attending a show, visiting a booth, or scanning a QR code does not automatically enter a vendor prize draw and does not improve the odds. Each open vendor draw has an equal alternate free entry method that requires no purchase, ticket, admission, VIP status, show attendance, booth visit, or QR scan. That method is draw-only and does not create booth-visit or QR Bingo progress. One valid entry is allowed per eligible couple per named vendor draw regardless of entry method. The Vendor Prize-Draw Official Rules govern those draws.

The named vendor is the vendor-promotion sponsor, operator, and prize provider and is solely responsible for lawful and accurate promotion and prize terms, eligibility and winner-release decisions, the mathematical skill-testing question, restrictions, taxes, claims, disputes, delivery, and fulfilment. Wedding Win acts only as the technical platform and administrator for the named vendor's promotion. It is not the named vendor-promotion sponsor, operator, or prize provider and does not supply, guarantee, insure, or fulfil that vendor prize. Wedding Win remains responsible for its own technology, privacy, security, representations, administrative conduct, and non-waivable duties. Apple is not a sponsor or participant.

3. Contact information and communications

Wedding Win Inc. may use ticket information to issue and validate the ticket, administer admission, provide event updates, respond to support or safety matters, and send other operational communications necessary for the requested event service. If a person separately chooses to contact a vendor or enter that vendor’s draw, Wedding Win Inc. may send the operational messages needed to complete that request or notify a potential winner.

Niagara Wedding Show ticket consent. By requesting, reserving, purchasing, accepting, receiving, holding, or using any Niagara Wedding Show ticket—whether free, paid, advance general admission, VIP, complimentary, promotional, or purchased at the door—the ticket holder expressly agrees and consents that Wedding Win Inc. may collect, use, and disclose the contact information supplied for that ticket, including the holder's name, email address, telephone number, wedding date if provided, ticket type, event details, and related consent record, to all participating exhibitors, vendors, and sponsors identified for that Niagara Wedding Show. Wedding Win Inc. and those participating exhibitors, vendors, and sponsors, acting in their own names, may use the information for show administration and follow-up, wedding-planning information, and their own wedding-related news, offers, and promotions.

This ticket-based contact sharing is separate from vendor prize draws. A ticket does not enter the holder in a draw, improve the odds, or place the holder on a named vendor's draw-entrant list. A draw remains an optional, separate choice governed by the Vendor Prize-Draw Official Rules. Each participating exhibitor, vendor, and sponsor is an independent sender responsible for its own communications and privacy practices. It must identify itself, protect the information, use it only for the disclosed event and wedding-related purposes, comply with applicable privacy and commercial-message laws, and provide and honour a working unsubscribe or consent-withdrawal method. Withdrawal does not reverse a disclosure already completed and does not stop transactional, security, ticket, safety, draw, or other requested-service communications that are permitted or required by law.

4. Entry, conduct, and safety

Attendees must present a valid ticket when requested, follow venue and staff instructions, comply with applicable law, and behave safely and respectfully. Harassment, threats, violence, intoxication that creates a safety risk, property damage, fraud, code manipulation, unauthorized resale, or other unlawful or disruptive conduct may result in refused entry or removal. Reasonable accessibility requests should be made as early as possible using the event contact information.

Attendance at a public event involves ordinary risks associated with crowds, travel, communicable illness, vendor demonstrations, and personal property. Attendees are responsible for taking reasonable precautions and supervising minors or other persons in their care. This paragraph does not waive any duty that applicable law does not allow Wedding Win or a venue to exclude.

5. Event changes, cancellation, and refunds

Event schedules, programming, vendors, capacity, and venue details may change for operational, safety, legal, weather, public-health, or other reasonable causes. Wedding Win will provide material updates using the available ticket contact information or event page. If Wedding Win cancels an event in its entirety, the refund or credit rights for a paid ticket will be the rights disclosed at checkout and required by applicable law. A free ticket has no refundable cash value. Nothing in this section removes a non-waivable consumer right.

Exhibitors and vendors are independent businesses, not Wedding Win employees, agents, partners, or franchisees. Wedding Win does not become a party to an attendee’s purchase, booking, contract, advice, product, or service agreement with a vendor. Attendees should evaluate vendor offers and terms before entering a separate agreement. Each vendor remains responsible for its own staff, booth, demonstrations, representations, goods, services, privacy and marketing practices, insurance, legal compliance, and customer obligations.

7. Photography and recordings

Events may be photographed or recorded for security, news, archival, and promotional purposes. Signs or other reasonable notice may identify filming areas. Crowd and background images may incidentally include attendees. Where practicable, an attendee who does not want to be the identifiable focus of promotional material should notify event staff. Wedding Win will obtain any additional consent required by law for a materially different use.
