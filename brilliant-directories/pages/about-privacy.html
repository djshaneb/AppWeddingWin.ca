WeddingWin Privacy Policy

Version: 2026-09-01

Effective and last updated: September 1, 2026

1. Scope and accountability

This Policy applies to WeddingWin.ca, the WeddingWin iOS app, member accounts, vendor listings, event admission and tickets, private messaging, QR Bingo booth-visit progress, optional vendor prize draws, notifications, support, and related APIs and technical services. Wedding Win Inc. is responsible for personal information under its control and for its own technology, security, privacy practices, and representations.

For a vendor prize draw, the named vendor is the promotion sponsor, operator, and prize provider. The named vendor is solely responsible for lawful and accurate promotion and prize terms, eligibility and winner-release decisions, administering the mathematical skill-testing question, prize restrictions, taxes, claims, disputes, delivery, and fulfilment. Wedding Win acts only as the technical platform and administrator for the named vendor's promotion; it is not the named vendor-promotion sponsor, operator, or prize provider and does not supply, guarantee, insure, or fulfil the vendor's prize. Wedding Win remains responsible for its own technology, processing, security, privacy, representations, administrative conduct, and every non-waivable duty or liability. It does not disclaim or transfer those obligations.

2. Information we process

  • Account and profile information: name, email address, phone number, account type, company or display name, wedding date, location, profile or listing details and images, and WeddingWin or service identifiers.
  • Authentication and service information: Apple or Google identifiers and information those providers make available, session or exchange records, consent version and time, device or browser information, IP address, security events, diagnostic records, and support requests.
  • Event admission and ticket information: depending on the ticket offer and checkout used for an event, this may include the event, free or paid admission type, ticket, order, reservation or confirmation identifiers and status, attendee contact details, price, taxes or fees, transaction status, and limited payment-confirmation metadata that Wedding Win receives. A separate ticketing or payment provider may process payment account or card details under its own privacy terms. QR Bingo and vendor-draw forms do not request payment-card details.
  • Messages and safety information: message text, participants, thread and message identifiers, timestamps, delivery and read state, retained historical media, reports, blocks, and moderation records.
  • Notifications: push token, device platform, account link, enabled state, delivery ticket or receipt, retry state, and errors. Generic message notifications are designed not to include private message text.
  • QR Bingo: decoded participating-vendor identifier, event, signed-in member link, scan time, duplicate state, booth count, and card progress. Camera frames used to decode a QR code are not uploaded by the audited iOS flow.
  • Vendor prize-draw entry: named vendor and event, contact information, entry method and time, rules version and consent record, named-vendor marketing acknowledgement and time, age-of-majority, residency and exclusion confirmations, prize terms linked to the entry, duplicate state, selection and verification state, and notice or fulfilment records. A keyed one-way value derived from normalized email is stored separately to prevent another chance through a different method without exposing an email lookup list.

3. QR scans, paid admission, and alternate free entry

A QR scan records booth-visit and Bingo-card progress only and never enters a draw automatically. A person may separately opt into a named vendor draw after reviewing the current rules or may use the public alternate free-entry route. General admission is free when obtained in advance while the free allocation remains. VIP admission is paid. Anyone without an advance general-admission ticket must purchase admission at the door. No purchase, ticket, admission, VIP status, show attendance, booth visit, or QR scan is required to enter a vendor draw through the equal alternate free method, and none creates an extra entry or improves the odds.

The alternate free-entry page displays and binds the request to one currently open vendor draw and its immutable vendor, event revision, prize, value, restrictions, region, dates, odds, and rules version. The form collects the information needed to identify the couple, apply account and normalized-email duplicate controls, administer the one-valid-entry-per-eligible-couple rule, confirm eligibility and rules acceptance, provide the named vendor the accepted entrant information expressly authorized for that draw and the vendor's own wedding-related marketing, and contact a selected potential winner. Its original submission time is preserved separately from the later administrator reconciliation time. It creates a draw-entry request only. It never records a booth visit, adds a scan, fills a Bingo card, or gives Bingo credit. These technical duplicate controls do not by themselves establish that different accounts or email addresses belong to the same couple. The named vendor makes the final duplicate and eligibility decision for a selected potential winner under the Official Rules.

4. Why we use information

Wedding Win uses information to:

  • create, authenticate, secure, and support accounts;
  • provide profiles, listings, planning features, and member content;
  • issue and validate free or paid tickets, administer admission, record the transaction status made available to Wedding Win, provide event updates, and process any applicable cancellation, refund, or support request;
  • synchronize and deliver private messages and generic notifications;
  • investigate reports, enforce blocks, prevent abuse, and protect users;
  • record QR Bingo booth visits and card progress;
  • technically administer separate optional vendor draws, reconcile duplicates, preserve consent, select a potential winner, record and support the named vendor's verification attestation, deliver notices after that attestation is recorded, provide the named vendor the accepted entrant information authorized for that specific draw, preserve fulfilment records, and address platform-record or technical disputes. The named vendor remains responsible for prize, eligibility, verification, delivery, and fulfilment disputes;
  • maintain security and reliability, diagnose errors, and comply with law;
  • respond to support and privacy requests; and
  • send marketing only where permitted and with any consent required by law.

5. Vendor access and named-draw entrant lists

When a person affirmatively enters a named vendor draw under the current draw-specific consent, Wedding Win may provide that named vendor a protected entrant list containing the person's name, email address, phone number if provided, wedding date if provided, and entry/consent evidence for that specific draw and the named vendor's own wedding-related marketing. Entry/consent evidence may include the event, vendor, participant reference, entry time and method, rules version, recorded eligibility-attestation status, and selection status. These are participant attestations, not Wedding Win eligibility certifications. The list excludes WeddingWin member identifiers and raw database identifiers. A booth scan, ticket, show attendance, or declined draw offer does not authorize this disclosure. The authorization applies only to the vendor whose draw the person explicitly entered.

Wedding Win records limited audit metadata about report generation, such as event and vendor, requesting vendor account, request time, app or website source, row count, and the applicable draw-administration sharing scope. Report contents and entrant identifiers are not stored in that report-generation audit.

The public QR results page may show a temporary, freshly reshuffled Participant N label with aggregate scan count, progress, and completion status. It does not show a stable participant identifier, name, contact detail, booth list, exact scan time, or vendor-draw entry status, and its temporary labels are not intended to identify or track a person between refreshes.

6. When we disclose information

Wedding Win discloses information only as reasonably needed to:

  • operate WeddingWin.ca and its directory, hosting, database, authentication, email, push-notification, ticketing, payment, security, logging, and support services, where those services are used;
  • allow Apple or Google to process a sign-in request under the user's provider settings;
  • disclose each accepted entrant's name, email address, phone number if provided, wedding date if provided, and entry/consent evidence to the named vendor that operates the draw, to administer that specific draw and, under the current explicit acknowledgement, for that vendor's own wedding-related offers and promotions;
  • comply with law, lawful process, safety needs, fraud prevention, or the establishment or defence of legal claims; or
  • complete a lawful business transaction subject to appropriate safeguards.

Once the named vendor receives entrant information, the vendor is responsible for its own lawful handling, safeguards, retention, access controls, and deletion of that information. The vendor may use it to maintain entry records, review eligibility and duplicates, contact a potential winner, meet promotion recordkeeping obligations, fulfil the prize, and send its own wedding-related offers or promotions under the entrant's current draw-specific acknowledgement. The vendor must identify itself, safeguard the information, provide and honour applicable unsubscribe or consent-withdrawal methods, stop future marketing when consent is withdrawn, and comply with applicable privacy, consumer, competition, and commercial-message laws. Wedding Win remains responsible for the disclosure decision and for personal information that remains under Wedding Win's control.

The named vendor must perform eligibility and skill-testing checks and obtain any required winner declaration or release outside WeddingWin. Wedding Win records the vendor's dated method/evidence attestation and verification result; it does not independently certify that participant document.

7. Marketing and commercial messages

Scanning a booth code or completing a Bingo card does not authorize Wedding Win to disclose contact information to a vendor and is not consent to receive marketing from Wedding Win or a vendor. If a person affirmatively enters a named vendor draw under rules version 2026-09-01-vendor-marketing, the displayed draw-specific acknowledgement authorizes Wedding Win to share the listed contact and consent details with that named vendor and authorizes that vendor to use them for the draw and its own wedding-related offers and promotions. Wedding Win does not send marketing on the vendor's behalf merely because the person entered. Commercial electronic messages must identify the vendor and provide a functioning unsubscribe method where required. A person may withdraw consent to future vendor marketing without losing an otherwise eligible draw entry; operational messages needed to administer that requested draw may still be sent where permitted.

WeddingWin requires a signed-in couple to complete a name, working email address, and usable phone number before recording QR Bingo booth visits. The scanner notice explains that a booth visit and a vendor draw entry are separate. Contact details are not provided to a vendor merely because its booth code was scanned; draw-administration sharing occurs only after the person separately chooses to enter that named vendor's draw under the current draw-specific consent. Entries accepted under an older rules version are not retroactively treated as vendor-marketing permission. A named vendor needs a new, explicit current-version acknowledgement before using a legacy entrant's information for marketing.

8. Retention

Wedding Win retains information only as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described here, including account operation, ticket and transaction administration, message delivery and shared history, draw administration and fulfilment, security, fraud prevention, dispute handling, and legal obligations. When information is no longer needed, it is deleted, de-identified, or allowed to expire from backups under applicable operational schedules, unless a limited legal or safety hold requires longer retention.

The keyed duplicate-prevention value described above expires 24 months after the applicable scheduled draw. It may remain after account deletion during that limited promotion-audit and fraud-prevention period, but it cannot be used as a public email lookup. Vendor responsibility acceptances are preserved as append-only audit snapshots so disabling a draw cannot erase the agreement that applied to entries already received.

9. Security and service providers

Wedding Win uses safeguards appropriate to the information, including encrypted transport, authenticated requests, access controls, protected credential storage, restricted administrative access, and audit records. Wedding Win limits service-provider access to the information needed for the assigned role and uses contractual or other safeguards intended to protect it. No system can be guaranteed completely secure.

WeddingWin and its service providers may process information in countries other than the user's province or country. Information may be subject to the laws and lawful access requirements of those locations. WeddingWin.ca may use cookies or similar storage needed for sessions, preferences, security, reliability, and any separately disclosed analytics or consented advertising functions.

10. Choices, access, correction, and deletion

Subject to applicable law, a person may request access, correction, deletion, or a copy of personal information, withdraw consent where processing depends on consent, disable notifications, unsubscribe from marketing, or ask how information has been used or disclosed. Identity verification may be required. A lawful exception will be explained where applicable.

A signed-in iOS user may choose About → Delete Account. Deletion removes or disables account-owned active records where permitted and closes related conversations. The other participant's copy of shared message history and limited moderation, security, draw verification, fulfilment, dispute, legal-hold, and backup records may remain for the limited purposes and periods described above.

11. Children, Apple, and changes

WeddingWin vendor prize draws are limited to people who have reached the age of majority in the eligible region and confirm that status before entry. Apple Inc. is not a sponsor, operator, prize provider, or administrator of a vendor draw. Apple processes app distribution, device, push, or sign-in information under Apple's own terms and privacy policy where those services are used.

Wedding Win may update this Policy to reflect product, legal, or operational changes. The revised version and effective date will be posted, and additional notice or consent will be provided when required by law.
