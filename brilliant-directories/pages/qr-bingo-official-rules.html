WeddingWin QR Bingo Official Rules

Rules version: August 28, 2026

No purchase is necessary. A purchase will not improve the odds of winning. QR Bingo scanning and each optional prize draw are separate. Filling a QR Bingo card does not automatically enter a prize draw.

Material draw disclosures shown before entry

The in-app entry screen is part of these rules and must conspicuously show the specific prize, its approximate retail value in Canadian dollars, the eligible Canadian region, the entry closing time, the scheduled draw time, and the odds basis before the entry control is enabled. If any one of those items is missing, entry for that draw is not open.

No purchase necessary. Each eligible entry has an equal chance and odds depend on the number of eligible entries received. A selected entrant is only a potential winner until they correctly answer a mathematical skill-testing question and satisfy the eligibility checks.

Alternate free method of entry

A promotion is not open unless its entry screen displays a functioning alternate free entry link. That route must permit an eligible person to submit the same required information and eligibility attestations without purchasing anything, attending the wedding show, or scanning a QR code, before the same displayed closing time. Alternate entries receive the same odds and are subject to the same one-entry limit and verification rules. If the link is absent or unavailable, the promotion is closed and entries must not be accepted.

Administrator, sponsors, and prize providers

Wedding Win Inc. administers and co-sponsors promotions offered inside the WeddingWin app. For a vendor-specific draw, the vendor identified on the entry screen is the prize provider and is solely responsible for supplying and fulfilling the prize described on that screen. The app records the vendor, prize description, approximate retail value, eligibility region, entry/draw timing, odds basis, free-entry route, eligibility attestations, and this rules version when an entrant opts in. Those material terms cannot change after the first entry; a different prize requires a new promotion version.

A QR Bingo grand-prize promotion is not open unless the app displays the configured prize, prize provider, entry period, eligibility requirements, and an active entry confirmation. Wedding Win Inc. is the sponsor and administrator of an enabled in-app grand-prize promotion; the provider named on its entry screen supplies or fulfills that prize.

Apple disclaimer

Apple Inc. is not a sponsor of and is not involved in any WeddingWin QR Bingo promotion, its administration, winner selection, or prize fulfillment. Apple is not responsible for any prize, claim, or dispute.

Eligibility and entry

Entry is limited to eligible persons in the Canadian region displayed on the entry screen who have reached the age of majority in their province or territory of residence, and is void where prohibited. Before entry, each person must separately attest to age of majority, residency in the displayed region, and that none of the exclusions below apply. Wedding Win Inc., participating prize providers, their employees, and members of those employees' immediate households are not eligible for a promotion they administer or supply.

An eligible person may use either the normal QR flow or the displayed alternate free entry route. In the normal flow, a vendor entry follows that vendor's scan and a grand-prize entry follows completion of the current QR card; both still require opening these rules and affirmatively confirming a separate entry. The alternate route does not require attendance or a scan. One entry per person/couple per displayed draw is permitted. Automated, duplicate, bulk, fraudulent, or incomplete entries are void.

Entry period, prize, and odds

The event name, entry opening and closing time, draw availability, prize description, and prize provider shown in the WeddingWin app at entry are incorporated into these rules. Vendors must provide an accurate prize description, approximate retail value in Canadian dollars, restrictions, expiry, and fulfillment instructions before accepting entries. Prizes are non-transferable and have no cash alternative unless the applicable prize provider expressly states otherwise. Odds depend on the number of eligible entries received for that draw.

Winner selection, verification, and notification

After the displayed entry period closes, WeddingWin's draw service selects a potential winner at random from eligible entries and records the result. A potential winner is not confirmed until eligibility is verified and any legally required declaration, release, and correctly answered mathematical skill-testing question are completed within the time communicated in the winner notice. If a potential winner cannot be contacted, is ineligible, declines, or does not complete required verification, another eligible entry may be selected. No final-winner email, public claim, or prize award is permitted until Wedding Win's service-controlled record shows both eligibility and skill-testing verification.

Contact sharing and privacy

Wedding Win Inc. uses entry information only to administer QR Bingo, prevent abuse, verify a potential winner, fulfill the prize, and preserve legally required records. Entrant lists and contact exports are not provided to a vendor. Only a selected potential winner's name and contact information may be disclosed to that prize provider, solely for verification and prize fulfillment. Marketing use is prohibited without separate, specific consent.

General conditions

Wedding Win Inc. may reject entries affected by fraud, tampering, technical failure, or rule violations and may suspend, cancel, or modify a promotion where permitted by law if it cannot be administered fairly as intended. The decisions of the applicable administrator are final, subject to governing law. Questions may be submitted through WeddingWin.ca's published contact channel.
