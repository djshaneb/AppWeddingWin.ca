WeddingWin Vendor Prize-Draw Official Rules

Rules version: 2026-09-01-in-person-entry

Effective and last updated: September 1, 2026

Enter at the wedding show

General admission is free when obtained in advance while the free allocation remains. VIP admission is paid. Anyone without an advance general-admission ticket must purchase admission at the door. Paying for VIP or door admission never creates an extra entry or improves the odds.

Vendor draws are for eligible couples physically attending the wedding show. After visiting a participating booth, scan that vendor's QR code and separately choose whether to enter. The QR entry is the digital replacement for a paper ballot. Scanning records booth-visit and Bingo-card progress, but never enters a person in a draw automatically. No purchase from the named vendor is required to enter.

1. Scope and vendor-specific disclosures

These rules govern only the optional vendor prize draws made available through WeddingWin's QR Bingo workflow. They do not open or govern any other promotion. A different or future promotion requires its own configuration, disclosures, rules, consent, and entry confirmation.

Before entry is accepted, the vendor-specific disclosure presented with the QR entry flow must identify the named vendor, one winner for that draw, the exact prize and its restrictions, approximate retail value or maximum savings in Canadian dollars, eligible region, opening and closing date and time with time zone, scheduled draw date and time, entry limit, odds basis, skill-testing-question requirement, the entrant information shared with the named vendor for draw administration and the vendor's own wedding-related marketing, and a link to these rules. If a required fact is missing, that vendor draw is not open.

2. Sponsor, operator, prize provider, and technical administrator

The vendor named in the vendor-specific disclosure is the promotion sponsor, operator, and prize provider for that vendor draw. The named vendor is solely responsible for lawful and accurate promotion and prize terms, for owning or having authority to provide the prize, for eligibility and winner-release decisions, for administering the mathematical skill-testing question, and for prize restrictions, taxes, claims, disputes, delivery, service, quality, and fulfilment.

The business name and WeddingWin vendor-profile contact route shown with the offer identify the named vendor. The vendor must keep that profile accurate, identify the correct legal or operating business, and provide a working route for prize and promotion questions throughout the entry and fulfilment period.

Wedding Win Inc. acts only as the technical platform and administrator for the named vendor's promotion. Wedding Win provides the entry interface, duplicate controls, rules and consent record, the pool of accepted entry requests, random potential-winner selection, verification state, and notice tooling on behalf of the named vendor. Wedding Win is not the named vendor-promotion sponsor, operator, or prize provider, does not supply, guarantee, insure, or fulfil the vendor's prize, and does not assume the vendor's promotion obligations. Wedding Win remains responsible for its own technology, security, privacy, representations, administrative conduct, and every duty or liability that applicable law does not permit it to exclude or transfer.

3. Apple disclaimer

Apple Inc. is not a sponsor, operator, prize provider, or administrator of any WeddingWin vendor prize draw and is not involved in entry, winner selection, verification, or prize fulfilment.

4. Eligibility

Entry is limited to an adult named for an eligible couple who has reached the age of majority in their province or territory of residence, resides in the eligible Canadian region shown for the named draw, and satisfies every vendor-specific condition. Entry is void where prohibited. Wedding Win Inc., the named vendor, their employees, and members of those employees' immediate households are not eligible for a draw they administer, operate, sponsor, or supply.

5. How to enter at the show

An eligible couple attending the wedding show may receive only one valid entry per named vendor draw. To enter, the couple must:

  1. visit the participating vendor's booth in person;
  2. scan that vendor's QR code while signed in to the couple's WeddingWin account; and
  3. open the separate prize-draw offer, review the current rules and disclosures, complete the eligibility confirmations, and affirmatively choose to enter before the closing time.

The in-show QR entry replaces a paper ballot and is the only current entry path through WeddingWin. A scan without the separate entry choice is not an entry. WeddingWin applies account and normalized-email duplicate controls. Using another account or email, or otherwise submitting more than once, does not create another valid chance. Automated, duplicate, bulk, fraudulent, late, or incomplete entries are void and may require another potential winner to be selected. The named vendor makes the final duplicate and eligibility decision before a winner is confirmed.

6. Prize, period, and odds

The vendor-specific disclosure is incorporated into these rules. The named vendor must provide an accurate prize description, approximate retail value or maximum savings in Canadian dollars, restrictions, expiry, delivery or booking requirements, and fulfilment instructions before entry opens. For a percentage-off prize, the vendor must identify the qualifying service or package, the percentage discount, the maximum dollar savings or discount cap, the approximate maximum value in Canadian dollars, expiry, booking requirements, and material exclusions. Unless the vendor-specific disclosure says otherwise, a prize is non-transferable and has no cash alternative. Each accepted entry request has an equal chance in the random potential-winner selection. Odds depend on the total number of accepted entry requests received for the named vendor draw. Every selected entrant remains only a potential winner until the named vendor independently verifies eligibility and the other requirements below.

7. Random selection, verification, and notice

After the displayed entry period closes, WeddingWin's technical service selects one potential winner at random from the eligible accepted-entry pool on behalf of the named vendor and records the result. Selection never sends a winner email automatically. A selected entrant is only a potential winner until the named vendor independently verifies eligibility, the entrant correctly answers one short mathematical skill-testing question without assistance, and any lawful declaration or release is completed. The named vendor—not Wedding Win—must administer those checks, obtain any required declaration or release outside the platform, and retain any supporting records. When the vendor chooses Send winner email, a confirmation asks the vendor to affirm that the required checks are complete. Wedding Win records the signed-in vendor's confirmation, its time, and the selected draw reference before sending the notice. This is a record of the vendor's confirmation, not an uploaded declaration, independent review, or certification of the entrant's eligibility or release.

Why the question is used: the draw first selects a potential winner at random. As a Canadian promotional-contest compliance safeguard, that person must correctly answer one short mathematical skill-testing question without assistance before being confirmed as a winner or receiving the prize. The question is not part of entry and does not change the odds. The named vendor administers the question and checks the person's answer outside WeddingWin. Wedding Win Inc. records the vendor's attestation that the required check was completed; it does not administer the question, evaluate the answer, or certify the result. The vendor remains responsible for administering the question and operating its promotion lawfully.

Each draw has one winner. A couple confirmed as the winner cannot be selected again for that same vendor's current draw. This does not exclude the couple from another vendor's draw. The couple remains in the vendor's complete entrant CSV and the audit record.

If the potential winner cannot be contacted, is ineligible, declines the prize, or the named vendor disqualifies that person after an incorrect answer or incomplete verification, another potential winner may be selected at random from the remaining accepted entry requests, subject to the same vendor verification. Use Choose a different winner before confirming the current selection; you do not need to enter a reason in the app or website. The previous selection and contact details are kept. If no other eligible couple is available, the current selection stays unchanged. Choosing another potential winner does not send an email. A final-winner notice, public announcement, or prize fulfilment must not occur until the vendor has completed the required eligibility and skill-testing checks and recorded its attestation. The named vendor is responsible for delivering the confirmed prize without undue delay and according to the disclosed terms.

8. Privacy, entrant lists, and named-vendor marketing

Wedding Win uses entry information to administer the named draw, reconcile duplicates, prevent abuse, select a potential winner, support the named vendor's verification, support notice and prize fulfilment, respond to disputes, and preserve records required by law. A booth scan alone does not disclose the couple's contact information to a vendor and is not permission for vendor marketing.

By affirmatively entering a named vendor draw under this version of the rules, the entrant agrees that Wedding Win may disclose the entrant's name, email address, phone number if provided, wedding date if provided, wedding venue if provided, and entry/consent evidence to that named vendor to administer that specific draw and for the vendor's own wedding-related offers and promotions. Entry/consent evidence may include the event, vendor, participant reference, entry time and method, rules version, recorded eligibility attestations, and selection status. These are participant attestations, not Wedding Win eligibility certifications. WeddingWin member identifiers and raw database identifiers are not included in the vendor download.

The named vendor may use this information for draw administration, including maintaining entry records, reviewing eligibility and duplicates, contacting a potential winner, complying with promotion recordkeeping, and fulfilling the prize, and for its own wedding-related marketing accepted with the entry. The vendor must identify itself, safeguard the information, provide and honour applicable unsubscribe or consent-withdrawal methods, stop future marketing after a valid withdrawal, and comply with applicable privacy, consumer, competition, and commercial-message laws. Withdrawal from future marketing does not invalidate an otherwise eligible entry. Wedding Win does not send marketing on the named vendor's behalf merely because a person entered. See the WeddingWin Privacy Policy.

This marketing authorization applies only to entries accepted under rules version 2026-09-01-in-person-entry. An entry accepted under an older rules version is not retroactively converted into marketing permission; the named vendor must obtain a new, explicit current-version acknowledgement before using legacy entrant information for marketing.

Before a potential winner is pending, an authorized named vendor may mark an entry excluded from random selection for a documented duplicate-entry or eligibility reason, and may restore it. This does not delete the entry or remove it from the vendor's complete entrant CSV or audit record. Entry-list changes stay locked while a potential winner is waiting for review.

10. Narrow prize release and preserved rights

To the extent permitted by law, a confirmed winner who voluntarily accepts a prize releases the named vendor and Wedding Win only from claims arising solely from the ordinary and disclosed risks inherent in accepting and using that prize. This release does not excuse the vendor from supplying or fulfilling the promised prize, does not excuse Wedding Win from its own technology, security, privacy, or administrative conduct, and does not apply to fraud, wilful misconduct, or any liability or statutory right that cannot lawfully be excluded or waived.

11. General conditions

Wedding Win may reject entries affected by fraud, tampering, technical failure, duplicate submission, or rule violations and may suspend or cancel a vendor draw when reasonably necessary to protect fairness, security, or legal compliance. The vendor may edit the prize title, description, and value until the winner email starts sending. Those prize details stay locked while the email is sending and after it has been sent. Eligibility, event dates, entry limits, and the other draw terms remain locked after the promotion opens or receives an entry. The vendor may still close entries. These rules are subject to applicable federal and provincial law, and nothing in them limits mandatory consumer, privacy, competition, or other non-waivable rights.

Questions about the prize or its fulfilment should be directed to the named vendor. Questions about WeddingWin's platform administration, technology, or privacy may be sent to info@weddingwin.ca or through the WeddingWin contact page.
