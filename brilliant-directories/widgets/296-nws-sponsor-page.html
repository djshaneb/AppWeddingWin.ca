Sponsor questions

A few helpful answers before you reach out.

If your idea does not fit neatly into one opportunity, send it anyway. The strongest partnerships are shaped around genuine guest value.

Who is a good fit for sponsorship?

Regional companies, venues, and wedding-related businesses with a useful prize, product, service, or show-experience contribution are encouraged to inquire.

What kinds of contributions are considered?

Current opportunities include featured gifts, door prizes, service-based offers, guest-bag products, and in-kind show features such as florals or decor.

What is the difference between sponsoring and exhibiting?

Sponsors contribute to the guest or show experience. Exhibitors apply for space to present their business on the show floor. If you want a booth, use the exhibitor application page.

When are contributions due?

Exact quantities, deadlines, and delivery instructions are confirmed after approval. Apply early so there is time to coordinate the right placement.

Does sponsorship include attendee follow-up?

Participating sponsors may receive eligible contacts shared under the show’s ticket consent. Each sponsor is responsible for lawful communication, privacy protection, and honouring opt-out requests.
